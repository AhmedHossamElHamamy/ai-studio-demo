Answers patient questions and collects appointment requests, 24/7

Two versions of the same assistant, side by side. Both answer only from the clinic's own information and send every appointment request to the clinic's email.

Standard

Built in engine. No API key needed.

No running cost

Try:

  • Answers hours, location, services and FAQ from the clinic's file
  • Understands typos and common wording ("insurnace", "braces", "where r u")
  • Never makes up an answer: if it isn't in the file, it says so and offers to take the visitor's details
  • Collects name, phone or email, and preferred day and time, then emails the clinic
Running costNone. Runs entirely in the visitor's browser.

AI version

Understands anything the patient writes.

Requires an AI API key

Try:

  • Understands any wording, follow up questions, and other languages
  • Still answers only from the clinic's information. Replies with prices or numbers that aren't in the file are blocked automatically
  • Picks up name, phone and email from a sentence and skips those questions
  • If the AI is slow or down, the built in engine answers instantly
Needs your own API keyConnect your own key from Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic Claude. Usage is billed by your AI provider at their prices. Example: Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite costs about $0.70 per 1,000 patient messages.

Install

Works on any website

Live in five minutes, without changing the clinic's website.

  1. Edit one fileClinic details, hours, services and FAQ in clinic-config.js.
  2. Upload two fileschatbot.min.js and clinic-config.js to the website.
  3. Paste one lineChoose how the chat appears, copy the code, done.
WordPressWixSquarespaceWebflowShopifyAny HTML site

A floating chat button in the corner of every page. Paste before </body>.

<script src="/chatbot/chatbot.min.js"
        data-config="/chatbot/clinic-config.js" defer></script>

The chat inside a section of a page, for example "Book online" or "Contact us".

<div id="chat" style="height:600px"></div>
<script src="/chatbot/chatbot.min.js" data-target="#chat"
        data-config="/chatbot/clinic-config.js" defer></script>

A full page chat with its own link. Share it on Google Business Profile, Instagram, emails or a QR code at the reception desk.

https://yourclinic.com/chat.html
